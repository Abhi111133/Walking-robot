01 — The project

Teaching a machine to take a step.

Walking is a balancing act repeated every half-second: shift weight, lift a leg, swing it forward, catch the fall. Our Makerspace project builds a robot that does exactly that.

⚙️

Mechanical design

Legs, joints and a frame designed and fabricated by the team.

🧠

Control

A controller coordinates every joint so the legs move in a repeating walking pattern.

🔋

Power & integration

Actuators, electronics and power brought together into one working machine.

02 — How it walks

One stride, four phases.

The cards below light up in sync with the robot in the hero. They follow its left leg through a general walking cycle.

PHASE 01

Stance

The foot is planted and carries the robot's weight.

PHASE 02

Push-off

The leg drives backward, moving the body forward.

PHASE 03

Swing

The knee bends and the foot lifts and travels forward.

PHASE 04

Landing

The leg extends and the foot touches down for the next stance.
